# Game accessibility checklist

Use during design and QA. Each item is from the Game Accessibility Guidelines (GAG) or WCAG 2.2. Source: `references/sources.md`.

## Vision

- [ ] No essential information conveyed by a fixed color alone (GAG)
- [ ] High contrast between text/UI and background (GAG)
- [ ] Contrast of at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (WCAG 1.4.3)
- [ ] Readable default font size, and an option to adjust it (GAG)
- [ ] Text resizes to 200 percent without loss of content (WCAG 1.4.4)
- [ ] Option to hide all non-interactive elements (GAG)
- [ ] Clear indication of which elements are interactive (GAG)
- [ ] Option to remove any difference between controller movement and camera movement (GAG)

## Hearing

- [ ] No essential information conveyed by sound alone (GAG)
- [ ] Customizable subtitle and caption presentation (GAG)
- [ ] Visual indication of who is speaking (GAG)
- [ ] Separate volume controls or mutes for effects, speech and music (GAG)
- [ ] Stereo/mono toggle (GAG)
- [ ] Visual means of communicating in multiplayer (GAG)

## Motor

- [ ] Remappable controls (GAG)
- [ ] Alternatives to holding buttons down (GAG)
- [ ] Controls as simple as possible, or a simpler alternative (GAG)
- [ ] Avoid repeated inputs such as button mashing and quick time events (GAG)
- [ ] Adjustable control sensitivity (GAG)
- [ ] Toggle or slider for haptics (GAG)
- [ ] Precise timing not essential to gameplay; offer alternatives (GAG)
- [ ] Assist modes such as auto-aim and assisted steering (GAG)
- [ ] Touch targets of at least 24 by 24 CSS px, or 44 by 44 where possible (WCAG 2.5.8, 2.5.5)

## Cognitive

- [ ] Interactive tutorials (GAG)
- [ ] All narrative and instructions can be replayed (GAG)
- [ ] Current objectives shown or recallable during play (GAG)
- [ ] No flickering images or repetitive patterns (GAG)
- [ ] A wide choice of difficulty levels, changeable during play (GAG)
- [ ] Autosave and manual save (GAG)
- [ ] Pre-recorded voice-over for all text, including menus (GAG)
