- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses (Morgan Kaufmann/Elsevier, 1st ed., 2008), ch. 14 (interest curves, L#61) - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: hook, rising interest with short rests, larger finale; plot per game, level and challenge; flat stretch is a defect; too much early interest dims later peaks - checked 2026-11-14
- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design (1st ed., 2008), ch. 11 (balance type 2, challenge, L#31) - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: very easy first levels; raise difficulty after each success with tension and release; playtest novices and experts - checked 2026-11-14
- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design (1st ed., 2008), ch. 11 ("Dynamic Game Balancing") - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: three problems with dynamic difficulty (breaks fixed world, exploitable, players want to improve by practice) - checked 2026-11-14
- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design (1st ed., 2008), ch. 11 ("Game Balancing Methodologies") - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: change a number by doubling or halving, not 10 percent, to learn the direction fast - checked 2026-11-14
- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design (1st ed., 2008), ch. 12 (puzzles, L#48-52) - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: obvious goal, easy start, show progress, gradual difficulty, parallel puzzles, pyramid structure - checked 2026-11-14
- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design (1st ed., 2008), ch. 16 (indirect control, L#71-73) - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: players need the feeling of freedom; six methods: constraints, goals, interface, visual design, characters, music - checked 2026-11-14
- Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design (1st ed., 2008), ch. 19 (space) - https://shop.elsevier.com/books/the-art-of-game-design/schell/978-0-12-369496-6 - supports: five space structures (linear, grid, web, points in space, divided space); landmarks; uniform order or chaos is dull - checked 2026-11-14
