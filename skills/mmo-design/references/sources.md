# Sources

All pages opened 2026-11-14. Book claims were checked against the free CC BY-NC-ND copy of Bartle's text and against reading notes for the printed chapters.

- Richard Bartle, Designing Virtual Worlds (New Riders, 2003, ISBN 0-13-101816-7), ch. 3 "Player Types", "Soft Role-Playing", "Community", "Things to Demote" and the character-slot discussion; ch. 4 "Closed or Open Economic Model?", "Economics", "Interference in Economies", "Tips for a Successful Virtual Economy", "Reset Strategy"; ch. 5 "Groups", "Opposition", "Levels", "Caps", "The Elder Game", "Testing a Design"; ch. 6 "Community Management" and "Economics" - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Designing_Virtual_Worlds (catalogue; free text at https://mud.co.uk/richard/DesigningVirtualWorlds.pdf) - supports: slot policy trade-off and SWG one-character rule; twinking and multi-lining; open versus closed economy; reward goods; duping; real-money trade options; group axes and multiple memberships; checks and balances; consent tools and the tailor problem; caps, remorting and the elder game; reset strategy; spreadsheets do not test fun - checked 2026-11-14
- Richard Bartle, "Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds, Spades: Players Who Suit MUDs" - https://mud.co.uk/richard/hcds.htm - supports: killers need achievers as prey; too many killers drive achievers away; four stable states, the balanced one included - checked 2026-11-14
- Raph Koster, "The Laws of Online World Design" - https://www.raphkoster.com/games/laws-of-online-world-design/the-laws-of-online-world-design/ - supports: faucet-and-drain economy; anonymity and empathy against griefing; killing players pays more than killing targets; ideal community size of at most 250; players find saving the world together more rewarding - checked 2026-11-14
- Raph Koster, "The Rules of Online World Design" - https://www.raphkoster.com/games/laws-of-online-world-design/the-rules-of-online-world-design/ - supports: keep a team on a persistent world forever; the Habitat vending-machine exploit; violence is inevitable and the issue is its form - checked 2026-11-14
- Brendan Drain, "EVE Evolved: ISK sinks and faucets" (Engadget, 2010) - https://www.engadget.com/2010/10/24/eve-evolved-isk-sinks-and-faucets/ - supports: faucet and sink definitions; the balance sets the inflation rate; CCP hired an economist in 2007 - checked 2026-11-14
- CCP, "Monthly Economic Report: December 2018" - https://www.eveonline.com/article/pkyphk/monthly-economic-report-december-2018 - supports: the report charts ISK float, velocity, price indices, sinks and faucets, production versus destruction - checked 2026-11-14
- Edward Castronova, "Virtual Worlds: A First-Hand Account of Market and Society on the Cyberian Frontier" (CESifo Working Paper 618, 2001) - https://www.ifo.de/DocDL/cesifo_wp618.pdf - supports: 29-item EverQuest price index fell from 100 (Q4 2000) to 71 (Q3 2001); equal-weight index - checked 2026-11-14
- CCP, "Fixing Lag: Character Nodes" - https://www.eveonline.com/article/fixing-lag-character-nodes/ - supports: load-balancing units on single-core nodes; hundreds of solar systems per location node; a system that exceeds one core cannot be balanced further; up to 80 percent of calls to Jita routed to character nodes - checked 2026-11-14
- CCP Veritas, "Introducing Time Dilation (TiDi)" - https://eveonline.com/article/introducing-time-dilation-tidi - supports: time runs slower under load to keep the task queue short; reinforcement timers do not dilate; cutting module cycle times rejected because it changes the game design - checked 2026-11-14
- Blizzard, "Realm Layering Update" (repost) - https://www.wowisclassic.com/en/news/realm-layering-update - supports: layering handles launch populations, is temporary, and layers per realm fell as players spread out - checked 2026-11-14
- Blizzard, "The War Within: Warbands Preview" - https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24061008/the-war-within-warbands-preview - supports: many players have several alts; account-wide versus character-specific; no intrinsic advantage for many alts; first-completion Warband reputation - checked 2026-11-14
- Livingston, Gutwin, Mandryk and Birk, "How Players Value their Characters in World of Warcraft" (CSCW 2014) - https://dl.acm.org/doi/pdf/10.1145/2531602.2531661 - supports: twenty interviewees; all kept alts; bank alts; ten characters per server and 50 per account at the time - checked 2026-11-14
- Jon Peters, "Jon Peters talks combat" (ArenaNet blog, 2011; archived copy) - https://web.archive.org/web/20120106162650/http://www.arena.net/blog/jon-peters-talks-combat - supports: healing slot, revive by every profession, boons stack in duration only, weapon swapping to change role; goals of dynamic, social combat - checked 2026-11-14
- Brian Green (Psychochild), "Rethinking the Trinity of MMO Design" (Game Developer, 2009) - https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/rethinking-the-trinity-of-mmo-design - supports: why the trinity persists and the costs listed - checked 2026-11-14
- Naoki Yoshida, "Letter from the Producer LXII" (2019-05-09) - https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/389675-Letter-from-the-Producer-LXII-(05-09-2019) - supports: two new jobs was the limit; Dancer added for physical ranged; healer balance difficulty - checked 2026-11-14
